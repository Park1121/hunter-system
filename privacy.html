2. In short

  • Step Up: SOLO is a game that turns daily life and training into quests.
  • There is no advertising and there are no analytics or tracking services.
  • Your body data (height, weight, year of birth, waist) stays on your device only. We do not receive it.
  • Your save and arena profile are stored on a server so you can find them on new devices and compete against friends.
  • You can delete your account and all data yourself at any time: Player → Status → Account → "Delete account and all data".

3. Which data we process and why

On your device. The app stores your save, settings, quests, history and your body data in the local storage of your browser or app. This is technically necessary for the app to work. We do not use advertising cookies. You enter body data voluntarily; it is only calculated locally and not transmitted. You can delete it at any time under Status → Body data.

Account and sign-in. You play either as a guest or with email and password. For guests, a random identifier is created. With an account, we store your email address and a hash of your password (not the password itself). For confirmation and password emails we use [[EMAIL DELIVERY: Supabase default or your own SMTP provider, please fill in]]. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Save on the server. When you play online, we store your save: name, level, experience, gold, stats, quests, history, settings, companions, achievements and, if you uploaded one, a small profile picture. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Arena profile. For rankings and duels we publish a profile with your name, level, rank, stats, consistency, companion team, achievements, records, weekly experience, friend list identifiers and your small profile picture. Other signed-in players can retrieve this profile. Please choose a nickname instead of your real name and do not upload a picture you do not want to share. You can switch off participation in the weekly ranking under Arena. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a working multiplayer part (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Duels and challenges. For these we store the identifiers and names of the participants, the stake, the status and the result.

Technical data when you open the app. When the app and the server connection load, your IP address, the time and browser details are technically generated. They are processed by the service providers named below for delivery and security.

5. How long we store data

Until you delete your account. With "Delete account and all data" we immediately remove your account, your save, your arena profile and your duels and challenges from the server. The provider's backups may still contain data for a short time until they are routinely overwritten.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw any consent at any time. You can do much of this directly in the app: change data, save a backup file of your save (machine-readable) and delete everything. For anything else, write to the email address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live. Responsible for the provider: [[ENTER COMPETENT SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY]].

7. Health data

Height, weight, year of birth, waist and gender are voluntary. They are stored and calculated on your device only. We do not receive them and do not pass them on. If you clear the browser or app storage, they are gone. Step Up: SOLO is a game and not medical advice.

8. Age

The app is intended for people aged [[MINIMUM AGE, e.g. 16]] and over.

9. Security

The connection to the server is encrypted. Access rules on the database let each player reach only their own data. The arena profile is intentionally visible to other signed-in players.
