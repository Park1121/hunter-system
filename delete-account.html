Delete your account and data

Step Up: SOLO · Deutsche Version

In the app (fastest way)

  1. Open Step Up: SOLO and go to Player → Status → Account.
  2. Tap “Delete account and all data”.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

This immediately removes your account, your save game, your Arena profile and your duels and challenges from the server. The local data on your device is deleted as well.

Without the app

If the app is no longer installed or you cannot sign in, email [[APP EMAIL ADDRESS]] with the subject “Delete account” and the email address of your account. After checking your request we delete your account, usually within 30 days.

What is deleted and what stays

  • Deleted: sign-in account (email, password hash), save game, Arena profile with profile picture, duels and challenges.
  • Kept briefly: the provider’s backups until they are overwritten as part of routine. Reports other players made about you may be kept longer for safety reasons. [[Have the retention period reviewed]]
  • Your subscription is not ended automatically. Also cancel it in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account.
