Contact

Questions, bug reports, cancellation or account help: stepup.solo@gmail.com

Please include your device (for example iPhone 15, iOS 18) and a short description of what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I cancel Premium? Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by the store that charged you (Apple or Google). We cannot refund payments ourselves.

How do I restore my purchase? Open the Premium screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple or Google account you bought with.

How do I delete my account and data? In the app, open the Player tab and tap “Delete account and all data”. More details: Delete account.

Is this medical advice? No. Step Up: SOLO is a game. Talk to a doctor before you start training if you have health concerns.
